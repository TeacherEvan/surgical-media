## Sources for the claims

1. About 85–90% of snake species worldwide are non-venomous, and most snakes bite only in defence when threatened or provoked.
   Source: https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change (citing StatPearls, Snake Toxicity; Frontiers in Psychology 2019)
2. Snakes eat rats, mice and other rodents; by controlling them they reduce crop losses and help limit rodent-borne zoonotic diseases. The George Institute notes grain destroyed by rodents annually could feed nearly 200 million people.
   Source: https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change (citing Singleton et al., Wildlife Research 2010)
3. Snakes are themselves prey for hawks, snake eagles, mongooses and other snakes (e.g. kingsnakes and king cobras), so removals ripple through multiple trophic levels.
   Source: https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change; https://savethesnakes.org/why-snakes
4. Snakes act as ecosystem engineers: seeds from eaten rodents pass through intact and are deposited farther away, aiding plant dispersal and restoration.
   Source: https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2018/02/snakes-act-ecosystem-engineers-seed-dispersal; https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change
5. Snake venoms gave rise to approved drugs including captopril (hypertension) and eptifibatide (acute coronary syndrome).
   Source: https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change (Table 1, citing Bordon et al. 2020; Mohamed Abd El-Aziz et al. 2019)
6. Snake populations are in widespread decline (Reading et al., Biology Letters 2010), while snakebite remains a serious health burden: up to 138,000 deaths and 2.7 million injuries per year (WHO).
   Source: https://www.georgeinstitute.org/our-impact/policy-statements-and-recommendations/snakes-the-ecosystem-and-us-its-time-we-change (citing Reading et al. 2010; WHO snakebite fact sheet)
